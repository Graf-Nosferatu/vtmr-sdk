## Vampire: The Masquerade — Redemption
All fixes are primarily intended for single-player mode and most of them require starting a new game
### Installation
Unpack the contents of the archive into the root folder with the installed game
#### Blood Pool
- Fixed: 'Christof' blood pool is now the same as that of the other party members
- Fixed: 'Christof' blood pool is correctly recalculated after turning into a vampire (if experience points had been invested in 'Faith')
#### Disciplines
- Changed: removed the additional cost for studying the first discipline in a group
- Changed: disciplines from ‘Common’ group can no longer be failed
- Changed: three disciplines 'Blood Strength', 'Blood Dexterity', 'Blood Stamina' are combined into one 'Blood Power'
- Changed: 'Walk The Abyss' discipline creates a mystic portal to Haven, and if this is not possible, it opens access Vault.
The initial blood cost for use has also been increased to justify leveling up the discipline.
- Changed: 'Fortitude' discipline now provides fixed protection (according to the formula). The full damage soak effect has been restored.
Increased the bonus at level 5. The duration no longer depends on the level and is equal to 1 minute.
#### Trading
- Changed: random trade goods replaced with fixed
- Fixed: the buy/sell price ratio (you can no longer buy cheap and sell high with a high 'Manipulation' rating)
- Added: trading is now conducted by the party player with the highest 'Manipulation' stat
- Changed: the start/end of trading has been significantly accelerated (the animation of the ‘Otto’ van plays once until the next reload or transition to another level)
